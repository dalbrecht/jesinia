About 120 px wide, the size windows reach with many open. Hue and shape alone still separate them.

What makes each one distinct

Notes

Sage band. Three columns: results, a wide pale reading page, and a chat rail with bubbles. The serif reading body shows as fine, even lines.

RSS

Marigold band. Classic three-pane reader and a dense item list. Chat only appears while engaged, so the usual silhouette has no chat.

Review

Plum band, tab row under it. A wide diff striped with green and red, and a narrative column with chat stacked underneath it, not beside.

Issues

Cornflower band. The only horizontal board: five columns of small cards, plus a triage drawer on the right. Compact density makes it the busiest texture.

Launcher and Settings

Neutral paper bands, so the launcher's four colored tiles stand out. Settings is a narrow form column with a left section list. Neither competes with the apps.
