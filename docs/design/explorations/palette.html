Palette directions

Each direction is a small family of tones, not one accent on grey. Primary drives buttons, links, and focus. Secondary handles reading highlights, markers, and the occasional warm touch. A label set of four or five muted tones covers tags and Issues labels. App hues appear only on launcher tiles and glyphs. Neutrals are tinted so surfaces feel like materials rather than plain greys. Selected rows get a soft fill and nothing else. Values are rough and get tuned to AA in Task 3.
